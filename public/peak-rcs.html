The 60-second explainer

What is rich messaging?

RCS is the successor to SMS: it lives in the phone's default messaging app, with no download, but behaves like a branded app. Vonage sends it through the same Messages API you use for WhatsApp, SMS and Viber.

1A verified sender

Your brand name, logo, colour and a verified badge on every message. Customers know it is really you, which lifts open and reply rates and cuts smishing risk.

2Rich cards and carousels

Photo, title, description and buttons in one card. Swipe through up to ten in a carousel: products, sessions, seats, offers.

3Suggested actions

One tap to call, open a map, add to calendar, share a location, or open a webview like this one without leaving the conversation. Every tap comes back to you as a postback, so you can track it.

4Delivered, read, replied

Delivery and read receipts, typing indicators and replies flow back over webhooks. If a handset has no RCS, Vonage sends the SMS version automatically.
